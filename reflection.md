# Reflection on Gen AI

## Gen AI Statement
Generative AI through Google Gemini, Shiny Assistant, and Google in general was used to help me complete this assignment in the following ways. I listed the things I Googled as well.
1. Looked up how to do a date range input.
2. Looked up how to make a checkbox that is optional.
3. Looked up how to do a tab.
4. Used AI to troubleshoot how to still have the bar chart if people do not select anything for the location option.
5. Used AI to add a validation to check if people manage to select a date outside of the data range.
6. Looked up how to angle the locations in the bar chart so they are readable.

## Reflection
I think AI is really helpful when trying to troubleshoot things that are going wrong in my code. It helps me figure out where I went wrong and what I can do next time to fix the issue on my own.
